# Writing the page

Every page has the same seven slots. Write them fresh for the subject — sensible, a little poetic, modern — and follow
any tone the user gave. When there's no brief, frame the page as a fictional exhibition, publication, event or
product launch built around the subject (it gives the CTA something to invite you to).

| Slot | Shape | Examples from the reference pages |
|---|---|---|
| **Title** | one word split in two, a pun or a re-reading of the word | Weight / *less* · un / fold · Hello, / world. · Up / *draft* · Bio / lume |
| **Spread sentences** | two short sentences, one word per span, spread across the full width; top states, bottom answers | "You don't need ground to stand on / You only need somewhere to look" · "Every poppy arrives folded like a letter / It takes one slow morning to read" · "In 1984 a beige box said hello / We have been answering ever since" |
| **Manifesto** | 1–2 sentences: a true, surprising fact + a turn toward the reader; 1–2 `<em>` accents; a `[01]` footnote | "A small cumulus weighs about as much as a hundred *elephants* — and still it floats, held up by warm air that *never stops rising.*" |
| **Footnote / sign-off** | the source of the fact + a wink | "[01] ≈ 500 tonnes of water droplets" · "Written looking up" · "Drawn at 72 dots per inch" |
| **Marquee** | 4 imperative verbs or short phrases with a separator glyph that fits the look | "Crumple · Unfold · Hold the light · Let go" · "Insert disk ■ Point ■ Click ■ Drag ■ Undo" · "Pulse ✶ Glow ✶ Drift ✶ Repeat" |
| **Specs** | 4 numbers with units; one should be a joke that still reads as data | "Things you need to be: None" · "Creases ironed out: 0" · "Brain 0 · Heart 0" |
| **CTA** | a two-line imperative (second line accented) + one line saying what the thing is + a button | "Come up / *for air.*" · "Open / *slowly.*" · "Say / *hello.*" · "Look / *up.*" · "Glow / *quietly.*" |

Rules:
- **Facts must be true.** Use well-known, checkable ones (the Macintosh 128K's 512 × 342 display, jellyfish having no
  brain or heart and 500 M+ years of history, ~500 t for a small cumulus as a widely quoted estimate). If unsure,
  phrase it as approximate or leave it out.
- Short words beat long ones in the title; check the split reads both as halves and as a whole.
- Labels and callouts in the line work are copy too: name real parts ("Stigma · a rayed disc", "3.5″ floppy · 400 KB",
  "Flat base · condensation level").
- Real brands and products: editorial description is fine, no logos, mark the page as a design study / fictional
  event in the footer.
- Keep the header CTA, the CTA button and the anchor ids consistent.
